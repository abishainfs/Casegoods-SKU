Quick guide: how SKUs are built

Our SKUs follow this format: X-ABCD-EF-GH-IJ

  • X – Category: e.g. F = Furniture.
  • ABCD – Product code: 3 or 4 characters, unique to each product.
  • EF – Size / type: 2 characters. Products without variations use 00.
  • GH – Primary material + finish (optional), e.g. T1 = Teak, Natural.
  • IJ – Secondary material + finish (optional).

The SKU updates as you choose. Click Save to history to keep a list you can copy or download.
